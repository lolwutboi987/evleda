# Candidate bill of materials

The CSV lists all 62 electrical references from the current authenticated contract and native board. H1-H4 are bare PCB mounting bores and are not purchased components. Values and footprint IDs come from the board; part candidates and their conditions come from the existing component-selection and circuit-input reviews. This is not a procurement or manufacturing release.

C20/C21 use the same selected TDK C3225X5R1A476M250AC part with the subsequently reviewed custom TDK land pattern. The earlier component-selection record named a stock footprint; that record is preserved and the difference is explicit in the CSV.

J2/J3/J4 have no selected fitted-header MPN. Their board contacts and pin assignments are defined; housing, mating header and mechanical fit remain assembly choices. The U1 design intent requires RP2350A A4 stepping. Retain the source reviews for capacitor bias/derating, regulator current/thermal limits, resistor dissipation and USB input/inrush conditions.
