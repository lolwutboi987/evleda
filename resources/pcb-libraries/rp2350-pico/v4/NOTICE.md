# v3 additions

The bare-contact footprint retains KiCad community electrical pad geometry under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license and design-output exception in provenance/KiCad-Libraries-LICENSE.md. Its name, bare-contact fabrication outlines, access courtyards and documentation are new. Stock plastic body, silk and 3D geometry are not represented. Wuerth switch and TDK bulk footprints, and the switch symbol, are independently authored from manufacturer facts. Manufacturer PDFs are not redistributed. Original v1/v2 attribution remains above.

# v4 mounting bore

The bare mounting-bore footprint and accompanying notes are original EvlEDA material based on the nominal hole dimension in Raspberry Pi's official Pico 2 drawing. No third-party CAD geometry or manufacturer PDF is redistributed for this addition. All inherited source/license notices remain applicable to their existing v1-v3 assets.
