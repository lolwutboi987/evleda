# Remaining selected stock sources

Reviewed 2026-09-17. Exact IDs and source identities are in designs/rp2350-pico/library-selection.json and the external qualification record. Stock files are referenced, not copied into this package. The actual bounded resolver checks every selected pin set.

- U1: MCU_RaspberryPi:RP2350A with QFN-60, 7 x 7 mm, 0.4 mm pitch, 3.4 x 3.4 mm EP. Preserve all 61 logical pins and native pad/paste features. Official RP2350 and retained Minimal pin maps govern functions; library selection does not replace core-power placement review.
- Q1: Transistor_FET:Q_NMOS_GSD gives 1 G / 2 S / 3 D. SOT-523 pads are 0.51 x 0.40 mm at x = +/-0.645; pins 1/2 are left at y = -/+0.5, pin 3 right at y = 0. These match DMG1012T suggested C = 1.29, X = 0.40, Y = 0.51 mm after rotation. Retain drain at divider and source at ADC for the intended body-diode orientation.
- D1: Device:D_Schottky and Diode_SMD:Nexperia_CFP3_SOD-123W replace the absent D_SOD-123W ID. PMEG6010ELR page 1 maps 1 K / 2 A with a cathode bar. Page 8 shows the nominal 2.6 x 1.7 mm body; page 9 shows 1.2 mm square reflow lands at 2.8 mm centers. Stock copper and left cathode marking agree. Stock paste follows the full 1.2 mm copper, while the vendor illustrates 1.1 mm paste and a 0.1 mm stencil; that difference remains an assembly-process review.
- Y1: Device:Crystal_GND24 with Crystal:Crystal_SMD_3225-4Pin_3.2x2.5mm. Abracon ABM8-272-T3 top view gives 1 lower-left, 2 lower-right GND, 3 upper-right, 4 upper-left GND. Stock numbering agrees. Lands are 1.4 x 1.2 mm at x = +/-1.1, y = +/-0.85, an explicit engineering land selection around package contacts, not an Abracon recommended PCB drawing. Stacked symbol GND pins 2/4 remain represented as two physical pads. Chamfer location alone does not identify pin 1.
- L2: Device:L with Inductor_SMD:L_Coilcraft_XxL4020. Pads 0.98 x 3.4 mm at x = +/-1.185 match the Coilcraft lands reviewed in component-selection. Use exact XFL4020-222MEC; the stock name does not qualify current/thermal behavior. L1 retains its exact marked Abracon footprint with Device:L.
- J4: Connector_Generic:Conn_01x03 with stock PinHeader_1x03_P2.54mm_Vertical, 1 mm bores and 1.7 mm copper. The plastic-body/courtyard representation stays intact. Its anchor is pad 1. Final fitted-header MPN and probe/mounting fit remain mechanical implementation work.
- R1-R20, C1-C19/C22-C26 and D2 retain the exact component-selection MPN/package choices and inspected 0402/0603 assets. D2 pad 1 is K/GND and pad 2 is A/LED_A. Passive numbers are CAD conventions; net membership is unchanged. C20/C21 use the new TDK lands.

Primary URLs remain in circuit/component-selection and package source-identities. This selection closes library-ID and pin-set structure. Electrical, assembly and physical qualifications remain separate from compiler readiness.
