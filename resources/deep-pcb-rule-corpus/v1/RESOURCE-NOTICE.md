# Deep PCB rule corpus resource notice

This directory is the application-owned, relocatable copy of the EvlEDA PCB
research corpus. The files under `docs/pcb-design-guides/` and
`generation/generate-deep-rule-assets.mjs` were copied byte-for-byte from the
2026-09-06 workspace corpus; their hashes are bound by
`resource-manifest.json`.

The dossiers are the evidence records. They retain their source ledgers,
publisher links, evidence grades, disagreements, scope limits, and warnings
about licensed standards. The JSON catalog and Markdown rule library are
derivatives for bounded retrieval; neither replaces those evidence records.

Linked third-party articles, standards, datasheets, tools, and other works are
not bundled here. Their publishers' copyright and license terms still apply.
This packaging does not grant a license to those linked works and does not
silently reproduce the non-public text of licensed standards. No separate
project-wide license was present beside the source corpus when this resource
was packaged; copying the corpus into the application does not change that
status.

Loading or selecting rules is engineering review support only. It never
authorizes fabrication, manufacturing, ordering, compliance, safety,
qualification, or release. Vendor numbers remain source- and process-scoped;
job-specific supplier confirmation and the required engineering gates still
control their use.
