# Physical trace topology

The V2 assessment can return `traceTopologyChecks` for every original routed-net
requirement. GND remains a plane net; this checker never substitutes a trace tree
for plane connectivity or infers component-internal connections.

The host collects the complete current native physical-pad inventory and native
endpoint evidence, then binds those observations to the saved PCB and actual V2
bundle. Exact source/native drill extraction includes circular holes and cardinal
slots. Normal through-vias connect their enabled copper layers, including inner
layers. Unsupported padstacks, offsets, geometry or exhausted work bounds remain
unproved rather than truncated into a successful result.

The numerical model uses rational nanometre coordinates. It constructs the
authored route spine, splitting at exact source junctions and qualified finite
pad/via contacts. Connected intrinsic copper bodies can join their actual contact
sites; merely sharing a pin number cannot join separate physical members.
Declared trees must be connected, acyclic, duplicate-free and terminate at pads.
Point-to-point routes additionally require the corresponding simple-path shape.

The complete finite copper-envelope contact inventory is checked separately.
Ordinary overlap around a bend is accounted for by exact common-point certificates
through already connected convex envelopes. An unaccounted contact cannot be
accepted merely because the centreline looks correct. Pad/via interiors, all
drills, board edges and positive copper witnesses are checked; intrinsic bore
regions do not become fabricated copper paths. The model does not measure current
capacity, minimum neck width, impedance or a general manufactured copper topology.

Public output preserves the source calculation, witnesses and limitations.
`sourceStatus: pass` requires complete source/contact evidence and native reachability
of every physical endpoint. The full original row still needs current qualified
native clearance/short checks. Missing fill/native-check authority leaves it
unknown. Definite authored loops and other supported source failures remain failed
even without that positive evidence.

This is an additional engineering check, not electrical or manufacturing approval.
Keep all unrelated requirements and unresolved findings visible.
